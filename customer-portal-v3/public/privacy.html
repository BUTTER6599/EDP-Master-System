THE ELECTRONICS DEPOT LLC

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 26, 2026

The Electronics Depot LLC respects the privacy of our customers and website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide to us, including your name, telephone number, email address, delivery or service address, appliance information, purchase information, repair information, warranty information, appointment information, and the content of messages or requests you send to us. We may also collect technical information related to use of our website or customer portal, such as device, browser, log, and security information.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to respond to questions, provide appliance availability information, process sales, arrange pickup or delivery, provide repair or warranty updates, schedule appointments or callbacks, send receipts and service notices, prevent fraud, maintain business records, improve our services, and comply with legal or regulatory requirements.

3. Text Messaging Information

When you provide consent to receive text messages, we may send customer-care messages related to appliance availability, purchases, pickup, delivery, repairs, warranties, appointments, callbacks, account notices, and other requested services. Marketing or promotional text messages will be sent only when the customer has provided the level of consent required for those messages.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You may reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Mobile information will not be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties except as necessary to provide the messaging service, comply with law, protect rights and safety, or when the customer directs us to do so.

4. Service Providers

We may use service providers for website hosting, communications, payment or point-of-sale functions, delivery coordination, data storage, analytics, security, customer support, and other business operations. These providers may access information only as needed to perform services for us and are expected to protect it.

5. Data Retention and Security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for business, service, warranty, accounting, legal, fraud-prevention, and compliance purposes. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no system can guarantee complete security.

6. Your Choices

You may ask to review, correct, or update certain information by contacting us. You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for assistance. Opting out of text messages does not prevent us from contacting you by another method when legally permitted or necessary to complete a requested transaction or service.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through our website or messaging program.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted with a revised effective date.
